# Open Repertory 1.37 — clinician workflow

Install through **Menu → App updates → Check → Download and install**, or use the version 1.37 GitHub release. Install over the existing app to retain its encrypted records and phone identity. The existing remembered-approval and device-locking policy is unchanged.

## Review why a remedy scored

Open **Analysis → Ranking → Why scored**. Each matched rubric shows its book, recorded grade and the importance selected for this case. Importance is a case setting; changing it does not rewrite the source grade.

Tap **Open rubric** beside the entry. The app opens that entry's own repertory and chapter, closes the phone drawer, filters to its rubric and highlights the exact saved path. It keeps the active case unchanged. **Back to analysis** or Android Back returns to the previous analysis view. Saved entries from removed repertories continue to show their retained source details instead of fetching a different book.

## Recover an accidental removal

In **Selected**, remove a rubric with its × button. **Undo** is available for ten seconds for the most recent removal. Confirmed **Clear all** also offers Undo. The original order, source identity, remedy grades and provenance are restored. Switching to another case does not redirect the restoration into that case. An explicitly re-added rubric keeps its current importance, and a deleted case is never recreated by an old Undo.

Clear all requires two taps for the currently displayed selection. Changing case or rerendering the selection resets its confirmation. If a save fails, the committed data remains intact; the controls refresh and the app reports that the change was not saved. A failed Undo can be retried through the same recovery action.

## Keep your preferred analysis view

Choose **Selected**, **Ranking** or **Matrix**. The selection is remembered on this phone along with the existing reading preferences. An empty case opens Selected while retaining the preferred view for populated cases. Text size, density, remedy visibility, glass effects and privacy preferences are preserved.

Source-review buttons and Undo use generous touch targets. Recorded grades 1–5 have readable badges in both themes, including enlarged text. Source navigation respects reduced-motion settings. In presentation mode, the generic Undo notice is available while ordinary potentially private notices and patient screens retain their existing protection.

## Synthesis and Complete Repertory

Neither is added in this update. Official products and documentation were researched, but no verified full export with APK redistribution rights was obtained. A reading program, demo or personal-additions file is insufficient to certify a complete imported database.
