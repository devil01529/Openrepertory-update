# Open Repertory 1.28

Open **Repertory**, then tap **Aa Repertory & reading** beside the repertory name above the chapters/rubrics. The button appears only on Repertory. This section is no longer in the main menu or Settings.

Use A− / A+ / Reset for text size; Show/hide remedies and Expand/collapse branches change reading display. Patient words closes the panel and opens the finder. Reading density & privacy opens the existing preferences. Glass effects controls translucent surfaces. Settings remain saved on this phone.

Close with X, Back or Escape. The panel scrolls on small screens. Reduced-motion preferences disable its entrance animation. Scores, patient records, sources, owner approval and updates retain their existing behavior.

Install this APK over the existing app; do not uninstall. Or use **⋮ → App updates → Check for updates**.
