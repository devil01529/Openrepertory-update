# Open Repertory 1.22 — clinical workspace guide

Install the update over your existing app. Do not uninstall or clear storage. The package and signing identity are retained; existing Apper approval remains required.

## Continue a consultation

**Today** puts the active consultation first. Resume opens its patient file, or the repertory/analysis for an unlinked case. New patient and New visit use the existing record and visit forms. Recent patients, cases and follow-ups are below.

The strip below the header identifies the patient and case. Tap it to choose another case or create a new one. Opening a patient's file does not silently attach the active case to that patient; the strip identifies when you are viewing a different file.

## Choose the right repertory

Open **Repertory**, then tap the current book name. **Kent · English (OOREP)** is the newly imported English Kent source. **Repertorium Publicum** is the separate book used by existing cases under the old internal `kent` key.

Existing cases retain their source lock. To analyse from English Kent instead of Publicum, choose English Kent and create a **new case** from the patient/case strip. Do not expect an existing Publicum case to accept Kent rubrics; mixing book grades would change its meaning.

Choose Whole book or Chapter beside the search field. The chapter drawer is available on phones; tablets retain a chapter pane. The patient-word finder is collapsed until you choose **Patient words** or Find rubrics from the menu.

Remedy lines show a readable preview. **Show all remedies** expands a line. **Source ⓘ** opens the rubric's original path, source IDs and available references. **Aa** changes Comfortable/Compact density, preview/all remedy lines and presentation mode. The same preferences are in **Tools → Settings → Reading & privacy**.

## Clipboard and analysis

After selecting a rubric, the floating control shows **only a clipboard icon**. Its accessible label includes the case's selected count. On short landscape screens it uses a reserved toolbar slot so it cannot cover Add controls.

Tap it to open Analysis. On phones choose **Selected**, **Ranking** or **Matrix**. Tablets show selected rubrics beside results. Weights and removal remain in Selected. Why scored explains a remedy's contributions. Matrix supports horizontal scrolling and a pinned remedy column. Save snapshot, history and comparison are in the action strip; copy/export/print are under More actions. Case and patient controls expand from the case summary.

Android Back returns through the workspace, including Analysis → Repertory and Notes → patient Overview. Menus and dialogs close first. Referenced rubrics also provide **Previous rubric**.

Scores and stored case formats are retained. Clarke still uses presence counts; printed italics are not strength grades.

## Patient file and library

Patient files have **Overview / Visits / Cases / Notes**. Editing Notes uses the existing saving and recovery mechanism. Visits retain their draft/retry behaviour. On a large tablet the patient list stays beside the file, and Notes can be viewed beside Cases.

Library provides remedy search, source-text filtering and comparison. Source text and alternate remedy names stay separate from **Your private notes**. Private remedy notes save on this phone and are included in encrypted backups. Same-remedy source refreshes preserve the reading position and note cursor.

## Presentation mode

Reading & privacy can hide patient/case identities in the shared context, patient screens and private remedy notes. The repertory and analysis remain available. Patient screens show a clear button to return to the clinical workspace. This is an on-screen presentation setting; exported records retain their original details, and it does not change the existing approval or device lock.

## Updates and owner access

The grouped Tools menu retains Account & access, Backup & restore and App updates. Owner approval management remains in the account surface. Use **App updates → Check for updates → Download → Install**, then confirm Android's Update prompt. Authentication, per-phone approval, signed metadata, APK hash/certificate verification and encrypted storage are retained.
