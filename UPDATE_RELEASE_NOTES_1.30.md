Open Repertory 1.30 — A cleaner section picker

Materia Medica’s Jump to section now opens an app-styled picker instead of the Android system dropdown. Search headings, see the selected section, and tap a row to jump directly to its source text. The list scrolls on small screens, supports enlarged text, and respects reduced motion.

Source passages, notes and clinical behavior are retained. Install over your existing app without uninstalling.
