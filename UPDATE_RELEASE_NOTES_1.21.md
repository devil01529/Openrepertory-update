Open Repertory 1.21 — floating Analysis shortcut

While browsing the repertory, select a rubric with + Add. A compact floating clipboard button labelled Analysis appears with the active case's selected rubric count. Tap it to open the existing full Analysis screen, including selected rubrics and remedy results.

The count follows the current case, saved selections and removals. The button hides for empty cases, on other screens, during text input, and while chapter drawers or dialogs are open. It sits above the bottom navigation, with scrolling space to reach the final rubric controls. Keyboard and screen-reader labels are included.

Clinical databases, source typography, scoring methods, encrypted records, Apper approvals and the signed updater are retained. Same package and Android signing certificate; no new permissions.

A separate major UI redesign plan proposes a consultation workspace, compact Today screen, improved patient/visit flow, structured repertory reading, dedicated analysis views and adaptive tablet layouts. That full redesign is planned, not implemented in this release. The accompanying visual concept uses synthetic data.
