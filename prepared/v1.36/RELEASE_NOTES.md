Open Repertory 1.36 — simpler source labels and faster remembered access

Provider labels now read “Open Repertory source.” Kent, Boericke and other book names remain visible. Original providers, authors, source records and complete licences remain available under Legal credits.

Verified approval is remembered securely for up to 30 days, including across phone reboots. The workspace opens from valid remembered approval while online checks run quietly. Connection failures or an expired hosted session do not delete or extend a valid remembered approval. Device locking, explicit sign-out, account mismatch and confirmed access denial still close access. Revocation cannot be checked offline or while the hosted session needs restoration; remembered access has a fixed expiry.

Today, saved-case analysis and patient tools open before the full repertory loads. Adds clear book-loading/retry states and fixes early Library statistics, rapid book-switch races, legal-notice retry and invalid-cache resume handling. Sign-out clears persistent cookies and cannot wait indefinitely for a busy clinical renderer.

Clinical content, remedy grades, source identities, chapter images and licences are retained. Same Android package and signing identity; no new permissions.
