Open Repertory 1.27 — glass-inspired clinical workspace

A coordinated glass interface for the header, navigation, tools menu, tabs, chapter selection, cards and dialogs. Light/dark/automatic themes, soft highlights, rounded controls and clear active states. Clinical reading surfaces stay opaque; source remedy grades and analysis values retain their original presentation.

New Glass effects switch in Menu → Repertory & reading. Turn it off for solid surfaces. Older Android WebViews use a readable solid fallback automatically. Reduced-motion and supported system contrast/transparency preferences are respected.

Restored chapter pictures, whole-drawer/Today scrolling, 80–140% text sizing, remedy visibility, branches, source selection, the clipboard shortcut, patient records, analysis, backups, owner approval and warm-session/device-lock protection remain available. Same package/signing identity, no new permissions. Install over your existing app; do not uninstall.
