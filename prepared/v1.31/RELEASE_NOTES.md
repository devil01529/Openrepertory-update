Open Repertory 1.31 — audited classical repertory expansion

Adds owner-authorized structured Boericke, BBCR and Allen 1891 Therapeutic Pocket Book datasets with recorded grades, aliases and explicit cross-references. Previous OCR editions remain available for saved cases. Kent databases are unchanged.

Preserves higher grades through analysis, snapshots and encrypted backups. Duplicate remedy links count once; conflicting grades and unsupported weights are retained for review and excluded from scoring. TPB is marked under development by its source. Complete API-record audit, not printed-book scan certification.

Phatak, Synthetic and Complete remain external catalogue entries pending authorized structured exports.
