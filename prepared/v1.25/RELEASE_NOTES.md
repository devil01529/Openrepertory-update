Open Repertory 1.25 — reliable Today scrolling and cleaner repertory navigation.

Today now scrolls to its final actions. Every book switch uses the same short-name picker. Chapters have a compact, searchable text index; selecting the current chapter keeps it open and closes the panel. Rubric branch arrows show child counts, with one-tap Collapse/Expand branches and deferred child rendering. Slow-start recovery now opens a late-ready workspace automatically. Remedy visibility, source records/grades, mixed-source cases, analysis and Apper approval are preserved.

Install over your current app; do not uninstall or clear storage.
